§1The Mirror Involution: Orthogonal Reflections, MIRIM Conjugation, and the Shadow Term

1.1 Two orthogonal mirrors and the Klein four-group of text

A line of text admits exactly two commuting involutions, one per axis of the glyph plane: σₕ (left-right reversal) and σᵥ (upside-down, the ambigram flip). The four maps {id, σₕ, σᵥ, σₕσᵥ} close under composition, every element is its own inverse, and σₕσᵥ = σᵥσₕ — the group is Z₂ × Z₂, the Klein four-group, and it sits at the top of the chain V₄ ◁ A₄ < A₅ that reappears in §4.4. mirim.py checks the composition table directly.

1.2 vert/virt/vort ⊥ natu/nets/norm: the vertical–horizontal split of the cognitive bundle

Take the principal bundle π: P → B of frames over the arena. The tangent space splits TₚP = Vₚ ⊕ Hₚ, and the two word-families name the two summands, one Winiwarter dimension at a time: vert/virt/vort tracks the fibre (the vertical, gauge, internal degrees of freedom — what can move without changing the base point) while natu/nets/norm tracks the connection's horizontal complement (what the world supplies as a frame, a net, a norm). Attention is a choice of horizontal subspace; relevance realization is the connection.

1.3 MIRIM: the conjugation operad

Lemma · one-way mirror at string level

mirror = MIR·ROR with σᵥ(mirror) = ROR·RIM: the palindromic block ROR transmits through reflection intact; MIR reflects into RIM, the frame. One half transmits, one half reflects-with-transformation — the string is a beam splitter, and its r-density is exactly 3/6 = 0.5, the reflectance of the half-silvered pane (lowercase r being the Fresnel amplitude reflection coefficient). The two chambers carry their own port labels: m-I-r | r-O-r.

1.4 The shadow term R′R: Maurer–Cartan and the gauge transformation law

The prime is deliberately bivalent, and both readings land:

(a) prime = adjoint. R′R = R†R, the Gram matrix — the metric a cast's shadow inherits. For a true reflection R†R = 1: a perfect mirror leaves no metric shadow. An oblique or lossy cast has R†R ≠ 1, and the shadow is the record of the distortion.

(b) prime = derivative. For a moving frame R(t), R′R† is the Maurer–Cartan form: skew-symmetric, and physically the instantaneous angular velocity. Under a gauge transformation R → gRg⁻¹, the shadow transforms exactly as a connection: R′R† → g(R′R†)g⁻¹ + g′g⁻¹ — the plaquette-holonomy conjugation law of lattice gauge theory, one loop at a time.

1.5 Clipping onto the relevance frame

The cast lands somewhere, and the landing has an industry name on each floor of the stack: softmax temperature, camera frustum, gauge fixing, attentional budget. Each is the same operation — a clip — applied to a different substrate. §4 asks what happens when the clip is iterated and fed by its own output.

§2Root Selection is Relevance Realization: The Jaeger Trialectic and the Solvable Mirror

C ≅ Φ(C), Φ(S) = S × ⌜S⌝ × ΩS

2.1 The cut is root selection

"A world is not received whole. It is cut into significance." A large world (Savage's term, adopted by the paper) is prestatable by no frame: features, partitions and relations are indefinite and situation-dependent. A free tree (A000055) is the framework's image of that condition — pure undirected relational structure, no privileged vertex, no orientation, no figure/ground. Relevance realization — delimiting an arena from a large world — is root selection: choosing the vertex from which every edge acquires direction (toward-root = mattering-gradient), turning the free tree into a rooted tree (A000081), i.e. the world into an arena.

2.2 The trialectic, mapped

The paper gives two triads with no first mover — a genuinely triadic junction (Thirdness, §0 junction arity: categories 5–6–7), not a composition of dyads. Agent, arena, and the affordance bracket ⟨norm|virt⟩ co-arise; none is prior.

2.3 The framing regress and the two fixed points

The paper's regress — frame(frame(frame(…))) → ? — is not climbed in the framework; it is solved as a fixed point, and there are exactly two canonical solutions, one per Winiwarter domain: the combinatorial fixed point T(x) = x·exp(Σ T(xⁿ)/n) for the tree domain, and the self-referential fixed point C ≅ Φ(C) for the discrimination domain.

2.4 The boundary of the model: Cantor blocks the total mirror; the clip solves it

Φ(S) = S × ⌜S⌝ × ΩS contains the discrimination factor ΩS. In Set, C ≅ Φ(C) would force a point-surjection C ↠ ΩC, which Lawvere's fixed-point theorem (the diagonal argument unifying Cantor, Gödel, Tarski) forbids for |Ω| ≥ 2.

Theorem · the boundary, made precise

No object of Set can satisfy C ≅ Φ(C) without collapse; the framework's escape is not to abolish the boundary but to clip it — evaluate Φ against a bounded, thermodynamically supplied norm (§1.5) rather than against the full unbounded ΩC. The clipped iteration stabilizes where the unclipped one diverges; mirim.py checks both behaviours side by side.

§3Cancelling the Self: ⌜S⌝ ⊗ ΩS ≅ 1 and the Price of Quotation

Mid-articulation move: S ≅ Φ(S) = S × ⌜S⌝ × ΩS ⟹ ⌜1⌝ ≅ 1/Ω ? Divide the fixed point by itself. If S may be cancelled, then

⌜S⌝ ⊗ ΩS ≅ 1 i.e. ⌜S⌝ ≅ Ω−S (at S = 1: ⌜1⌝ ≅ 1/Ω)

The question is what "divide" means, and the answer splits into three regimes — each one load-bearing.

3.1 Where cancellation fails, consciousness fits

In Set, S × K ≅ S does not imply K ≅ 1: ℕ × 2 ≅ ℕ by interleaving (Hilbert's hotel). Cancellation fails exactly for infinite — reflexive — S, and this failure is not a defect: it is the mechanism of §2.4. A reflexive state-space absorbs the factor ⌜S⌝ × ΩS without that factor being trivial; the room for self-encoding is created by the very infinity that breaks division. Finite minds cannot cancel; infinite ones need not. The fixed point lives in the gap.

3.2 Where cancellation succeeds: the conservation law of self-reference

Pass to any multiplicative measure — cardinality, Euler characteristic, generating function. If |S| is invertible, division is legal and forces

|⌜S⌝| = |Ω|−|S|

The Kraft–Shannon identity Σ 2−ℓ = 1 for a complete prefix code is the finite, computable shadow of this conservation law: a complete self-describing code spends its whole budget and no more.

4.2 The terminal limit: grammar failure, not goal failure

The clip of §1.5 — softmax, frustum, gauge fixing, attentional budget — is one application of N with the norm supplied from outside (§2.4: the metric on K₄ is thermodynamic input). The Spire is the clip iterated with the norm replaced by its own output: m′ depends on p′, so the affordance bracket ⟨norm|virt⟩ of §2.2 is evaluated against a norm that the previous evaluation wrote. The trialectic's holonomy — each circuit returns to a changed frame — is still present, but the change is always in the same direction: curvature with a fixed sign. That is the precise sense in which the Spire is "reciprocal": the vort of §1.2 has become a drift.

Definition · the two failures, made operational

Goal failure: ⟨p, f⟩ is poor although argmax f lies inside the ε-support of m — the tactic is expressible and merely wrong; feedback η repairs it. Grammar failure: argmax f lies outside the ε-support of m — the adequate tactic is not expressible in the remaining production rules, and no feedback η can move mass to it because the multiplicative update prices it at mκ ≈ 0. The terminal limit of the Spire is the moment goal failure can no longer be distinguished from grammar failure from inside: every anomaly is absorbed by further narrowing. Checks 4–5 exhibit the lock: after the payoff optimum jumps to the other sheet, the N-only field holds ⟨p, f⟩ ≈ 0.000 for the rest of the horizon while its detector reports grammar failure at every beat.

The detector is the framework's meta-cognitive layer with its Aion names — frame-lock detection, infinite-regress detection — and it has a clean signature: vanishing information gain (ΔH → 0) coinciding with rising anomaly cost. A system that can report only goal failure is inside its Spire; the ability to report grammar failure is the first countervail, and it is a report about the medium, not about the product.

4.3 Echo, Return, and the price of the fibre

Reopening needs information that the projection did not destroy. If repeated projection has produced a quotient π: X → X/∼, a section (a retained tag per orbit) or a lift is required; without it, quotienting is irreversible. The Echo is the operator that supplies the complement: for each frame, its antipode on the other sheet. The reciprocal opening is

O(P) = P ∪ ρ(P) ∪ B(P), ρ = complement injection (needs the tag), B = bounded branching along G

with two conditions the source reconstruction stated but did not enforce: O is budgeted — the mass leaving the incumbent equals a declared ερ + εB and nothing more (check 10) — and O acts on the medium m, not only on p (check 11). The second condition is the one that matters: reopening the product while the grammar stays terminally differentiated re-locks within one beat. Critical-period reopening is a reduction of the stabilising constraint, not the regrowth of every pruned synapse — the framework's version is that O writes λ of its opening back into m.

Theorem · Return is §3, and the tag costs ½

The cosmogonic Return ⊙⊙ = ○ — the distinction applied to itself dissolves to the void — is the conservation law ⌜S⌝ ⊗ ΩS ≅ 1 of §3.2 in the cosmogonic vocabulary: self-application cancels. It is not annihilation; it is the fixed point re-solved with the shadow term restored. The retained fibre tag is exactly one bit — H(lift) − H(base) = log 2 for uniform states (check 26) — and its groupoid measure is |B(ℤ₂)| = ½ (§3.3): the price of keeping the quotient liftable is the half-silvered constant. Compression is safe only when this bit has not been spent, and checks 6–7 show the two outcomes: with the tag retained, N·N·O recovers the shifted optimum within one N·N·O cycle; with the tag destroyed, the same schedule never recovers within the horizon, because B can only crawl the incidence graph the Spire has already priced to zero.

The sheet label itself is a gauge choice — which representative you keep per orbit — and only the holonomy around a cycle in the quotient is invariant. An even-holonomy cycle lifts to two disjoint closed walks however carefully the sheet bits were kept (check 23). The Echo therefore retains the tag; it cannot manufacture a twist. Where the twist comes from is the next subsection.

4.4 The twisted lift and the A₅ dichotomy

Theorem · ℤ₂ voltage lift

Let a base cycle of length n carry ℤ₂ voltages. Its lift closes after n steps if the holonomy is even and after 2n steps if it is odd, in which case the single lifted walk visits both sheets (check 13, 300 random trials). An odd-holonomy Hamiltonian cycle of an n-orbit quotient is therefore one closed 2n-step traversal of every state on both sheets — the cycle-extending countervail: exploitation of the quotient template plus an explicit traversal of the complement, closing once.

The framework's own instance. The 60 darts (v,w) of the icosahedron are the vertices of the truncated icosahedron and form a torsor of A₅ — the top of §1.1's chain V₄ ◁ A₄ < A₅. The antipode (v,w) ↦ (−v,−w) is a fixed-point-free automorphism; its quotient has 30 orbits and is cubic (checks 14–17). The dart reversal ρ: (v,w) ↦ (w,v) — the dagger of §1.1 — commutes with the antipode and together they realise the Klein four-group on the darts, but ρ is not a graph automorphism: it exchanges tails for heads, so it is not a covering involution of this graph (check 19). The double cover is the antipodal one.

4.5 Alternation: the echobeat as schedule

The Spire is (⊕t) ⊗ (⊕t) with one beat suppressed. Restoring the alternation gives the schedule that keeps every local Spire provisional; the minimal one is N·N·O, one echobeat of §0's 3 × 12 — two beats of convergence, one of opening, at 120° offset — and under it dim P never returns to 1 (check 8) while payoff recovers to within its shifted optimum through the shift. The eight-phase cycle of the source reconstruction is this schedule unrolled: phases 1 and 8 are N, phase 2 is the detector, phases 3–6 are O decomposed (Echo, Return, ρ, the other-sheet traversal), phase 7 is re-normalisation. The mature form is not an inverted Spire but the forest the Core Claim already names: rooted trees drawn and redrawn on a free tree whose other vertices were never deleted. After the shift, the field re-converges on the complement of the old tip (check 9: 0 → 12) — the top of the old hierarchy is the base of the new one, and the base was there all along as the fibre.
